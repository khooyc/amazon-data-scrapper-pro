## 🚀 5-Minute Setup

### Step 1: Extract the Files
Unzip the extension folder to your computer.

### Step 2: Load into Chrome/Edge/Brave
1. Open your browser
2. Go to `chrome://extensions/` (or `edge://extensions/` for Edge)
3. Turn ON "Developer mode" (toggle in top-right corner)
4. Click "Load unpacked"
5. Select the folder you just extracted
6. Done! The extension icon appears in your toolbar

---

## 🎯 Quick Test

1. Go to [Amazon UK](https://www.amazon.co.uk)
2. Search for "dumbbells" (or any product)
3. Click the extension icon in your toolbar
4. Select a few products → **Watch orange borders appear!**
5. Click "⚡ Extract Data"
6. Wait for scraping to complete
7. Click "📊 Download Excel" → **File downloads automatically!**
8. Open the file in Excel/Google Sheets

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I need to copy-paste anymore?**
A: Nope! Just click "Download Excel" or "Download CSV" and it saves automatically.

**Q: Will the orange borders stay on Amazon?**
A: They update in real-time as you select/deselect. If the page reloads, click "Rescan".

**Q: What if variant names still show as ASINs?**
A: Some products don't have variant labels on Amazon. The scraper will use ASIN as fallback.

**Q: Can I use this for research/business?**
A: Yes! The data is perfect for market research, competitor analysis, pricing studies, etc.
