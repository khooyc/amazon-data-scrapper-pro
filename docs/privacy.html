PRIVACY OVERVIEW

Your research stays under your control.

AMZ Scrapper Pro reads product information from supported Amazon pages only when you open and run the extension. This page is a plain-language overview; the repository-linked policy remains the canonical privacy policy.

What the extension accesses

Version 2.1 requests access to the active tab, supported Amazon domains, local extension storage, tabs, scripting, and clipboard writing. These permissions support page scanning, product selection, extraction, export preferences, and the Copy CSV action.

How processing works

Product information is read from pages you already have open. Extraction and file creation happen through the extension in your browser. Preferences can be stored locally in browser extension storage.

Your responsibilities

You decide which pages and products to process. Use the extension responsibly and in accordance with Amazon’s Conditions of Use and any laws or policies applicable in your jurisdiction.

Source and support

The extension source is available on GitHub. Read the canonical privacy policy or report a privacy concern through the project’s issue tracker. Never post private account or customer data in a public issue.
